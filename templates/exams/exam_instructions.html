1. Navigation & Palette Guide:
  • 1 Gray: You have not visited this question yet.
  • 2 Red: You have visited the question, but have not answered it.
  • 3 Green: You have selected an answer for this question.
  • 4 Purple: Marked for Review (no option chosen).
  • 5 Purple with Green dot: Answered and Marked for Review (will be evaluated).
2. Answering Questions:
  • Click on the option radio button or text card to select your choice.
  • Click Save & Next to securely save your answer and advance to the next question.
  • To change an answered option, simply click on another option and click Save & Next.
  • To deselect a chosen option, click Clear Response.
  • To mark a question for future review, click Mark for Review & Next.
3. Server Timer & Auto-Submit:
  • The server-synchronized countdown timer runs continuously in the top bar.
  • Auto-Submit: When the countdown timer reaches zero, the examination will automatically submit, regardless of client browser activity.
  • Ensure your responses are saved before the final minutes elapse.
4. Anti-Cheating & Proctoring Protocol:
Examination Proctoring Warning: This examination environment requests full-screen mode. Tab switching, minimizing the browser, copy-paste keystrokes, and right-click context menu actions are strictly monitored and logged into your examination audit profile. Repeated violations may be flagged for faculty review.
